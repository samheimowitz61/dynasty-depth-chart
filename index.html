A visual depth chart for your Sleeper leagues

See how your roster stacks up against a minimum-talent plan: 3 QBs, 8 RB/WR with at least 4 studs, and a starting TE in superflex. Every player gets a rating from production in your league's scoring plus current and future market value. Tap anyone for the full breakdown.

Data: Sleeper public API (rosters, stats, schedule) and FantasyCalc (market values; offense only, so IDP, K and DEF are rated on production). Ratings scale each metric 0–100 against the top players rostered in this league on that side of the ball, so QB, RB, WR and TE ratings compare directly. Tiers use league-wide rank on current ability (production + current value).
