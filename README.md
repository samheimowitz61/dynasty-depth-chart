# Dynasty Depth Chart

A phone-friendly, visual depth chart for Sleeper dynasty leagues.

- Live rosters, stats and schedule from the public Sleeper API; market values from FantasyCalc.
- Grades each position room against a minimum-talent plan derived from your league's starting slots
  (e.g. superflex: 3 QB, 8 RB/WR with 4+ studs, 1 starting TE).
- Ratings blend production in your league's scoring with current and future market value.

Open the site, enter a Sleeper username, and add it to your home screen. Nothing is stored anywhere but your own browser.
